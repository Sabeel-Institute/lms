Privacy Policy

Sabeel Institute LMS · last updated 16 October 2026

Sabeel Institute LMS is a private application used by the students and staff of Sabeel Institute to watch and listen to recordings of the institute’s own classes, and to set, hand in and grade assignments. It is not a public service, and you cannot sign yourself up: staff accounts are the institute’s @oursabeel.com Workspace accounts and must be approved by an administrator, and student accounts are created by a teacher.

What we collect

InformationWhere it comes fromWhy
Your name and email address Your teacher, when they set up your account. For staff, your Google Workspace account when you sign in To identify you on class registers, listening records and assignments
Which recordings you have played, how much of each, and where you stopped The app, as you watch or listen To return you to your place, and to record the listening the class requires
Your attendance, and whether you completed required listening Your teacher, and your own confirmation in the app It is the academic record of the class
Your work on assignments: your written answers, and the documents, photos and audio you attach You, in the app: typed, chosen from your device, or photographed with its camera when you choose to So your teacher can read it, grade it and return it to you

The class recordings themselves, audio and video Recorded by the institute, in class or on Zoom They are what the application exists to deliver
Whether this device may receive notifications, and a device token Your phone or browser, if you turn notifications on To tell you a recording is ready, a deadline is near, or your work has been returned
Crash and error diagnostics The app, automatically So faults can be found and fixed

Crash reports carry no name, no email address and no account identifier. This application attaches no user identity to them at all. That is enforced in the code, not by policy alone.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone for their own purposes.

Who else can see it

These service providers process this information on the institute’s behalf, under agreements requiring them to protect it to the same standard set out here and to use it only to provide their service:

  • Google — sign-in for staff accounts.
  • Google Cloud and Firebase — where the application and its database are hosted, in the United States.
  • Cloudflare — where the class recordings and the files attached to assignments are stored, in North America.
  • Zoom — where a class is taught on Zoom, the recording is made and held in the institute’s own Zoom account before it is brought into this application.
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics.
  • Apple — delivers notifications to iPhones, and receives the device token of an iPhone that has notifications turned on.

Who can see your work

Your answers, your files and your grades are seen by you, by the staff who teach your course, and by the institute’s administrators; never by other students. Work you have not handed in is yours alone until you hand it in. A grade reaches you when your teacher returns your work.

Who can watch or hear a recording

Recordings are not public and are not shared outside the institute. A student can open a recording only for a class they were excused from, and only until the date their teacher set; after that the recording closes. The links the app uses to play recordings and open files expire, so one cannot be passed on and used later.

How long we keep it

Attendance, required listening and completion, and the work handed in for assignments with its grades, are the academic record of the class. They belong to Sabeel Institute rather than to the individual, and are retained as institutional records — that remains true after somebody finishes or leaves a course. Class recordings are retained by the institute for as long as they are useful to it.

Your account is a different thing: your sign-in credentials, your contact details and your notification settings are removed when your account is removed.

Deleting your account

Email privacy@oursabeel.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm and act within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your ability to sign in, your email address wherever we hold it, and your notification settings. It does not remove the academic record described above, which keeps your name, as the institute retains any other record of what happened in a class.

You can also ask us at the same address what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it if it is wrong.

Children

This application is offered to adult students of the institute. It is not intended for and is not offered to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and tell account holders by email.
