Sabeel Institute LMS

Sabeel Institute LMS lets students of Sabeel Institute listen to recordings of classes they were excused from, and lets teachers keep the register and see who has listened. It is a private application for the institute, not a public service.

Getting an account

Accounts are set up by the institute; there is no way to sign yourself up.

Students: your teacher registers your email address, and you receive a link to set your own password. Sign in with that address and password.

Staff: sign in on this website with your @oursabeel.com account. An administrator then approves you — until they do you will see a “waiting for approval” screen, which lets you in by itself the moment they approve.

“This account isn’t set up for the app yet”

The phone app signs in existing accounts only. If you are staff and have never used Sabeel Institute LMS before, sign in on this website once first and the app will then work. If you are a student, check you are using the address your teacher registered.

Forgotten password

On the sign-in screen, enter your email address and choose Email me a password link. If nothing arrives, check the address your teacher registered for you.

A recording you expected is missing

You can open a recording only for a class you were marked excused from, and only until the listen-by date. After that date the recording closes and is marked missed. Your teacher can tell you which applies.

Contact

Email privacy@oursabeel.com for anything about your account, your information, or deleting your account. For anything about a class, its recordings or your attendance, contact your teacher.
