You can just use this website

Sabeel Institute LMS works in any browser, on a phone or a computer, with nothing to install. The app is worth having if you listen on a phone: it keeps playing when you lock the screen or switch to something else.

Sign in here first

Before you install the app, sign in on this website once. The phone app does not set up new accounts — it only signs in accounts that already exist. If you install it first, it may tell you the account is not set up yet, and this is why.

If you have already used Sabeel Institute LMS in a browser, you are done; go ahead and install.
