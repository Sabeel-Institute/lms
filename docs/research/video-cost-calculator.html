1 · Storage

Keeping every recording for a year

Video is added at the same rate every day of the week: the weekly hours divided by 7. Storage is billed on the average amount held over each month, so each month costs a little more than the one before. Nothing is deleted.

  • Prices are for a Cloud Storage bucket in us-central1, as used in the project's cost research: $0.020 per GB-month stored and $0.12 per GB downloaded. Google bills these in GiB, about 7% larger than a GB, so real bills run slightly lower.
  • The free 5 GB and 100 GB are shared by the whole project, including the audio recordings. Audio uses about 0.03 GB an hour.
  • A month is taken as 52 ÷ 12 = 4.33 weeks. The storage table uses the actual days in each month of 2027.
